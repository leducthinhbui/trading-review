What the record says about my trading

  • My recent exits were well timed. I sold memory, chip, bitcoin and gold positions in August and September 2026, before the October sell-off in AI and chip stocks.
  • Earlier, I sold winners too soon. In the 2025–mid 2026 rally I often took small profits and missed large moves, most clearly AMD and NVIDIA. Microsoft hurt twice: I sold at a loss near its low, six weeks before strong cloud earnings.
  • Buying after a drop beat the market but not my other buys. Some dip buys kept falling, such as Xero, Palantir and Sandisk, so a lower price alone is not a reason to buy.
  • What to do differently: keep part of a winning position instead of selling all of it, and check whether the business has changed before treating a fall as a chance to buy.
